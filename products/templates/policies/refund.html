Return & Refund Policy

1. Return Eligibility

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery for items that are damaged, defective, or incorrect. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and with all tags intact.

2. Refund Process

Once your return is received and inspected, we will notify you. Approved refunds will be credited back to your original method of payment or added to your Store Wallet Balance within 5-7 business days.

3. Non-Returnable Items

Certain hygienic essentials and perishable goods cannot be returned for safety reasons.
